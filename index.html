THE ANALYSIS

What goes into every pick

The app starts with the public race card, considers relevant Del Mar history, removes scratched horses, and watches the odds as post time gets closer. It then turns that information into one practical ticket.

1 MARKET

The market’s view of the race

Morning-line odds provide a starting point. Current odds help show whether the race has changed as post time approaches.

2 HISTORY

Relevant Del Mar history

Past Del Mar results for each horse, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and the post position on this surface and distance add context. A short track record is treated cautiously.

3 FIELD

Who is actually racing

Scratched horses come out of the comparison. The remaining field is checked for a horse, or a small group, that stands out.

4 LIVE

Late changes before post time

The app keeps checking the race as post time approaches. A preliminary pick can change when the odds, field, or other race-day information changes.

THE RESEARCH FOUNDATION

How research supports the algorithm

The model was developed using more than 1,800 archived Del Mar races with matched official results. The research evaluates every horse’s probability—not only whether the top-ranked horse won—and records the rules needed to reproduce each comparison.

DATASET

Race cards matched to results

An audit confirmed that all 1,851 archived races used in the research dataset matched an official result and a unique winner. The model uses public race information rather than private betting data.

SMALL SAMPLES

Short records are treated cautiously

Horse, trainer, jockey, pairing, and post-position history can add context, but limited experience is pulled toward a neutral baseline so a few races do not receive too much weight.

EVALUATION

More than top-pick accuracy

The research compares full probability estimates using established measures such as log loss and Brier score. It also keeps races from the same card together when resampling, reducing the chance that one unusual day looks like broad evidence.

NEXT TEST

The rules are frozen before the next test

The current model, historical-data rules, and analysis are versioned and frozen for future prospective monitoring. The formal sealed prospective evaluation has not begun, so the site does not call the model proven or profitable.

What the research does—and does not—establish

An independent adversarial audit found no direct current-outcome or future-history leakage and led to seven corrections. It also found that retrospective improvement over the prior model was not statistically established. That is why Horse Racing Companion shows recommendations beside official results and never claims a guaranteed winner, profit, or proven betting edge.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app execute or transmit wagers?

No. The app provides wagering advice: it may display an optional real-world ticket, let you adjust the suggested total up to $5, show a rough estimated return, and provide teller wording. It does not accept deposits or wagering funds, create a wagering account, hold a wagering balance or credits, execute or transmit a wager, connect to a betting operator, or operate a simulated betting game or virtual bankroll. Any wagering transaction and exchange of wagering funds occur entirely outside the app.

What does the season pass purchase?

The season pass unlocks analytical recommendations in the app. It is not a deposit, balance, wagering credit, virtual currency, or payment method for a wager.

Is recommendation confidence my chance of winning?

No. The confidence meter shows how clearly the available information supports the prediction. Steadier evidence fills more of the meter. It is not a predicted win percentage and does not guarantee an outcome.

What information does the analysis use?

The current prediction model ranks the active horses using public morning-line odds and past Del Mar results: each horse’s local record, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and post-position performance by surface and distance. Scratches determine who remains in the comparison. Closer to the race, current odds for the leading selection are checked and can affect the final ticket.

When do live recommendations begin?

The app starts checking live odds about two hours before the day’s first race and shows the expected opening time on the Races screen. Before then, Recommendations Open Later is the normal scheduled state, not a data error. If fresh information is unavailable after that window opens, the app switches to Live Updates Delayed and continues checking automatically.

How is the algorithm backed by research?

The model was developed using more than 1,800 archived Del Mar races with matched official results. Its full probability estimates are compared using established scoring measures, including log loss and Brier score, while short histories are pulled toward a neutral baseline. An independent adversarial audit found no direct current-outcome or future-history leakage and led to seven corrections. The audit did not establish that the current model is statistically superior to the prior model, and neither the research nor the app claims a guaranteed winner, profit, or proven betting edge.

Why is my recommendation preliminary?

Live odds, scratches, and race changes can alter the calculation. The status tells you what's still updating and when the recommendation is expected to be final.

Is this an official Del Mar app?

No. Horse Racing Companion is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Del Mar Thoroughbred Club.
