ANALYSIS OVER GUESSWORK

A bet built from public evidence—not superstition.

The model starts with what the public market and the Del Mar record are actually saying. It compares the active field, looks for meaningful separation or ambiguity, and selects one coherent single-race ticket.

MARKET

Morning-line and current odds

Available odds are converted into a normalized view of the active field, so every horse is compared on the same basis rather than as an isolated price.

HISTORY

Del Mar trainer and jockey results

Past performance by each horse’s connections is applied as a cautious, sample-adjusted signal—not as an automatic rule or a guarantee.

FIELD

Active runners and field clarity

Scratched horses are removed. The model then measures how clearly the leading contenders separate and how much uncertainty remains across the field.

LIVE

Odds, scratches, and race-day changes

The recommendation keeps checking current information as post time approaches, so a preliminary ticket can change before the final decision window.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you head to the track.

Does the app place bets?

No. Horse Racing Companion does not accept deposits, create a wagering account, transmit wagers, or connect to a betting operator. You decide whether to place a wager yourself.

Is “evidence strength” my chance of winning?

No. It describes data quality and recommendation stability. It is not a predicted win percentage and does not guarantee an outcome.

What information does the analysis use?

The current model uses public morning-line or live odds, historical Del Mar trainer and jockey results, the active field and scratches, and the amount of separation or ambiguity among the leading horses. It continues checking live odds and race-day changes before the decision is finalized.

Why is my recommendation preliminary?

Live odds, scratches, and race changes can alter the calculation. During the beta, every candidate also remains preliminary until the algorithm completes its prospective performance record and passes the release standard.

Is this an official Del Mar app?

No. Horse Racing Companion is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Del Mar Thoroughbred Club.
