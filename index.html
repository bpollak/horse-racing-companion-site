WHAT GOES INTO A PICK

A clear recommendation, built from the race in front of you.

The app starts with the public race card, considers relevant Del Mar history, removes scratched horses, and watches the odds as post time gets closer. It then turns that information into one practical ticket.

MARKET

The market’s view of the race

Morning-line odds provide a starting point. Current odds help show whether the race has changed as post time approaches.

HISTORY

Relevant Del Mar history

Past results for each horse’s trainer and jockey add context. A short track record is treated cautiously.

FIELD

Who is actually racing

Scratched horses come out of the comparison. The remaining field is checked for a horse—or a small group—that stands out.

LIVE

Late changes before post time

The app keeps checking the race as post time approaches. A preliminary pick can change when the odds, field, or other race-day information changes.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you head to the track.

Does the app place bets?

No. Horse Racing Companion does not accept deposits, create a wagering account, transmit wagers, or connect to a betting operator. You decide whether to place a wager yourself.

Is recommendation confidence my chance of winning?

No. Low, Medium, or High describes how clearly the available information supports the recommendation. It is not a predicted win percentage and does not guarantee an outcome.

What information does the analysis use?

The current model uses public morning-line odds and past Del Mar trainer and jockey results to rank the active horses. Scratches determine who remains in the comparison. Closer to the race, current odds for the leading selection are checked and can affect the final ticket.

Why is my recommendation preliminary?

Live odds, scratches, and race changes can alter the calculation. The status tells you what is still updating and when the recommendation is expected to be final.

Is this an official Del Mar app?

No. Horse Racing Companion is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Del Mar Thoroughbred Club.
