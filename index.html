THE ANALYSIS

What goes into every pick

The app starts with the public race card, considers relevant Del Mar history, removes scratched horses, and watches the odds as post time gets closer. It turns that information into one clear pick.

1 MARKET

The market’s view of the race

Morning-line odds provide a starting point. Current odds help show whether the race has changed as post time approaches.

2 HISTORY

Relevant Del Mar history

Past Del Mar results for each horse, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and the post position on this surface and distance add context. A short track record is treated cautiously.

3 FIELD

Who is actually racing

Scratched horses come out of the comparison. The remaining field is checked for a horse, or a small group, that stands out.

4 LIVE

Late changes before post time

The app keeps checking the race as post time approaches. An early pick can change when the odds, field, or other race-day information changes.

THE RESEARCH FOUNDATION

How research supports the algorithm

The model was developed using more than 1,800 archived Del Mar races with matched official results. The research evaluates every horse’s probability—not only whether the top-ranked horse won—and records the rules needed to reproduce each comparison.

DATASET

Race cards matched to results

An audit confirmed that all 1,851 archived races used in the research dataset matched an official result and a unique winner. The model uses public race information rather than private betting data.

SMALL SAMPLES

Short records are treated cautiously

Horse, trainer, jockey, pairing, and post-position history can add context, but limited experience is pulled toward a neutral baseline so a few races do not receive too much weight.

EVALUATION

More than top-pick accuracy

The research compares full probability estimates using established measures such as log loss and Brier score. It also keeps races from the same card together when resampling, reducing the chance that one unusual day looks like broad evidence.

NEXT TEST

The rules are frozen before the next test

The current calculation and historical-data rules are versioned and frozen before future testing begins. That formal test has not begun, so the site does not call the picks proven or profitable.

What the research does—and does not—establish

An independent review found no direct use of current results or future race history and led to seven corrections. It also found that improvement over the prior calculation was not statistically established. That is why Horse Racing Companion shows saved picks beside official results and never claims a guaranteed winner, profit, or proven betting edge.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app execute or transmit wagers?

No. The app labels each pick by type, such as Win, Place, or Show, shows pick confidence, and may estimate the total return for the track’s minimum stake using current odds. It has no adjustable stake control, teller wording, betting-window instructions, wagering account, deposit flow, wallet, credits, betting-operator connection, or wager execution or transmission. Any wagering decision and transaction occur entirely outside the app.

What does the season pass purchase?

The non-renewing season pass unlocks race-day picks for the Del Mar season and end date shown before purchase. The app and App Store show the exact price and covered period before confirmation. It never renews automatically and is not a deposit, balance, wagering credit, virtual currency, or payment method for a wager.

Is pick confidence my chance of winning?

No. The confidence meter shows how clearly the available information supports the pick. Steadier evidence fills more of the meter. It is not a predicted win percentage and does not guarantee an outcome.

What information goes into a pick?

The app compares the active horses using public morning-line odds and past Del Mar results: each horse’s local record, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and post-position performance by surface and distance. Scratches determine who remains in the comparison. Closer to the race, current odds for the leading horses are checked and can affect the final pick.

When do live picks begin?

The app starts checking live odds about two hours before the day’s first race and shows the expected opening time on the Races screen. Before then, Live Picks Open Later is the normal scheduled state, not a data error. If fresh information is unavailable after that window opens, the app switches to Live Updates Delayed and continues checking automatically.

How accurate are the tracked live picks?

From August 20 through August 22, 2026, 10 of 23 finalized pre-race picks matched the official result, an initial pick-match rate of 43.5%. Across 36 settled races, the app passed 13 times instead of forcing a pick. “Matched” is judged by the published pick type, and every pick is frozen before the official finish is known. At track minimum stakes, the 23 picks represented $47 in hypothetical total cost and $31.10 in official returns (-33.8% hypothetical ROI). This small initial sample does not establish profitability or a betting edge.

How is the algorithm backed by research?

The model was developed using more than 1,800 archived Del Mar races with matched official results. Its full probability estimates are compared using established scoring measures, including log loss and Brier score, while short histories are pulled toward a neutral baseline. An independent adversarial audit found no direct current-outcome or future-history leakage and led to seven corrections. The audit did not establish that the current model is statistically superior to the prior model, and neither the research nor the app claims a guaranteed winner, profit, or proven betting edge.

Why can my early pick change?

Live odds, scratches, and race changes can alter the calculation. The status tells you what is still updating and when the pick is expected to be final.
