THE ANALYSIS

What goes into every pick

The app starts with the public race card, considers relevant Del Mar history, removes scratched horses, and watches the odds as post time gets closer. It turns that information into one clear pick.

1 MARKET

The market’s view of the race

Morning-line odds provide a starting point. Current odds help show whether the race has changed as post time approaches.

2 HISTORY

Relevant Del Mar history

Past Del Mar results for each horse, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and the post position on this surface and distance add context. A short track record is treated cautiously.

3 FIELD

Who is actually racing

Scratched horses come out of the comparison. The remaining field is checked for a horse, or a small group, that stands out.

4 LIVE

Late changes before post time

The app keeps checking the race as post time approaches. An early pick can change when the odds, field, or other race-day information changes.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app execute or transmit wagers?

No. The app labels each pick by type, such as Win, Place, or Show, shows pick confidence, and may estimate the total return for the track’s minimum stake using current odds. It has no adjustable stake control, teller wording, betting-window instructions, wagering account, deposit flow, wallet, credits, betting-operator connection, or wager execution or transmission. Any wagering decision and transaction occur entirely outside the app.

What does the season pass purchase?

The non-renewing season pass unlocks race-day picks for the Del Mar season and end date shown before purchase. The app and App Store show the exact price and covered period before confirmation. It never renews automatically and is not a deposit, balance, wagering credit, virtual currency, or payment method for a wager.

Is pick confidence my chance of winning?

No. The confidence meter shows how clearly the available information supports the pick. Steadier evidence fills more of the meter. It is not a predicted win percentage and does not guarantee an outcome.

What information goes into a pick?

The app compares the active horses using public morning-line odds and past Del Mar results: each horse’s local record, its trainer and jockey, their pairing, and post-position performance by surface and distance. Scratches determine who remains in the comparison. Closer to the race, current odds for the leading horses are checked and can affect the final pick.

When do live picks begin?

The app starts checking live odds about two hours before the day’s first race and shows the expected opening time on the Races screen. Before then, Live Picks Open Later is the normal scheduled state, not a data error. If fresh information is unavailable after that window opens, the app switches to Live Updates Delayed and continues checking automatically.

Yes. Your first three racing days include complete race-day picks at no charge. Race cards, odds, official results, past picks, and the How It Works page remain free without a pass.

Why can my early pick change?

Live odds, scratches, and race changes can alter the analysis. The status tells you what is still updating and when the pick is expected to be final.
