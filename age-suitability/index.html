Race analysis for adults.

Horse Racing Companion covers adult horse-racing wagering subject matter. It is rated 18+ and is intended only for people of legal wagering age where they use it.

What the app does not do

No wager is executed or transmitted by Horse Racing Companion.

The app does not accept deposits or wagering funds, open a wagering account, hold a wagering balance or credits, connect to a betting operator, or operate a virtual bankroll. Any wagering decision and transaction occurs outside the app.

Use it responsibly

  • Use the app only if you are of legal wagering age and wagering is lawful where you are.
  • Set a firm entertainment budget before racing begins and never wager money needed for housing, food, healthcare, debt, or other essentials.
  • Do not chase a loss, increase a limit because of a losing result, or treat model confidence as certainty.
  • Take breaks and stop if wagering stops feeling voluntary or enjoyable.
  • Do not use the app if a racing authority, employer, team, ownership role, or venue rule restricts your wagering.

Every prediction can lose. No outcome, profit, or betting edge is guaranteed. Analysis confidence describes the clarity of the information available to the model; it is not your chance of winning.

Confidential help resources

If gambling is causing concern for you or someone you know, visit the National Council on Problem Gambling help and treatment directory or call or text 1-800-GAMBLER. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

For parents and families

The app is not directed to children. Apple Screen Time, Ask to Buy, purchase restrictions, and device-level controls can help prevent access or purchases by minors. Do not share purchase credentials with a child.
