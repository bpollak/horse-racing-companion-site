Recommendations and your decisions

Recommendations are estimates based on available information and model assumptions. Racing is uncertain. Data can be delayed, incomplete, incorrect, or change after a recommendation. A Low, Medium, or High confidence label describes the clarity of the available information; it is not a probability of winning. No recommendation guarantees a result or profit.

A preliminary recommendation can change as current odds, scratches, or other race information changes. A final recommendation may still lose. The app may show no bet when a field is unusually unclear or when missing, delayed, or changed data prevents a safe ticket.

You alone decide whether to wager, what to say to a teller, whether a ticket is correct before accepting it, and how much you can afford to lose. Always confirm the track, race, bet type, horse program numbers, base amount, combinations, and total on the issued ticket before leaving the window.
