Data delayed at the track?

Pull down on Today to refresh. If the app says live data is delayed, it hides current ticket instructions and does not use one of your free days.

Recommendation status

What do Preliminary, Final, and No bet mean?

Preliminary means current odds, scratches, or other race changes can still alter the recommendation. Final means the race-day calculation is complete. No bet means the field is unusually unclear or the app cannot safely provide a ticket.

Why does the app say Preliminary?

The recommendation is still being recalculated with odds, scratches, or other race changes. The status area tells you what remains and when the race calculation is expected to finish.

What does recommendation confidence mean?

The confidence meter shows how clearly the available information supports the prediction. Steadier evidence fills more of the meter. It is not the chance of winning, and a confident prediction can still lose.

Reading a ticket

Is the program number the horse number?

Yes. The program number is the number used on track screens and betting tickets. It is not a prediction that the horse will finish in that position.

What should I say at the betting window?

A final recommendation displays the teller-ready view. It provides the track, race, base amount, bet type, and program numbers in the standard order. You can read it aloud or show the screen to the teller.

Can the app place the bet?

No. The app cannot accept money, open a wagering account, place or transmit a wager, or guarantee that a teller accepts a ticket. You make every decision and transaction yourself.

Free days and season access

When is a free day used?

Only after you intentionally choose to unlock a Del Mar racing day while live information is available. That unlocks every race on that day’s card.

Is the app free?

Your first three racing days are free and include complete recommendations. After that, a one-time, non-renewing $19.99 Del Mar season pass unlocks recommendations for the rest of the season. Race cards, odds, official results, and the How It Works page stay free without a pass.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If no valid pass is found, email support with the app version and approximate purchase date. Never send payment-card information.

Report a problem

Email feedback@horseracingcompanion.com. Please include:

  • The race date and race number.
  • The app version and build from Settings.
  • What the screen said and what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain information you prefer to keep private.

Do not include an Apple Account password, payment-card number, government identification, or wagering-account credentials.

Privacy and accessibility help

For access, correction, or deletion requests, use the subject line “Horse Racing Companion Privacy Request” and include the Installation ID shown in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details. For an accessibility issue, read our Accessibility statement or email support with the screen and assistive technology involved.

Responsible wagering

The app is for adults of legal wagering age and only where wagering is lawful. Set a limit before the first race and never chase a loss. For confidential help resources, visit the National Council on Problem Gambling.
