Data delayed at the track?

Pull down on Today to refresh. If the app says live data is delayed, it suppresses current ticket instructions and does not use a complimentary day.

Recommendation status

What do BET, WAIT, and SKIP mean?

BET means a complete final ticket is ready. WAIT means live information still needs to settle. SKIP means the current evidence does not justify a ticket for that race.

Why does the app say Preliminary?

The candidate is still being recalculated with odds, scratches, or other race changes. During the beta, recommendations also remain preliminary while the live performance record is completed. The status area tells you what remains and when the race calculation is expected to finish.

What is evidence strength?

It describes data quality and recommendation stability. It is not the chance of winning. Strong evidence can still lose, and the app may still advise SKIP.

Reading a ticket

Is the program number the horse number?

Yes. The program number is the number used on track screens and betting tickets. It is not a prediction that the horse will finish in that position.

What should I say at the betting window?

Only a final BET displays the teller-ready view. It provides the track, race, base amount, bet type, and program numbers in the standard order. You can read it aloud or show the screen to the teller.

Can the app place the bet?

No. The app cannot accept money, open a wagering account, place or transmit a wager, or guarantee that a teller accepts a ticket. You make every decision and transaction yourself.

Complimentary days and season access

When is a complimentary day used?

Only after you intentionally choose to unlock a Del Mar racing day while live information is available. That unlocks every race on that day’s card.

Why can’t I buy the season pass?

Paid access stays unavailable until a wager family passes the full live release standard. The planned pass is a one-time $19.99 purchase for the dates named in the app and will not renew automatically.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If no valid pass is found, email support with the app version and approximate purchase date—but never send payment-card information.

Report a problem

Email brettcpollak@gmail.com. Please include:

  • The race date and race number.
  • The app version and build from Settings.
  • What the screen said and what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain information you prefer to keep private.

Do not include an Apple Account password, payment-card number, government identification, or wagering-account credentials.

Privacy and accessibility help

For access or deletion requests, use the subject line “Horse Racing Companion Privacy Request.” Read the Privacy Policy for details. For an accessibility issue, read our Accessibility statement or email support with the screen and assistive technology involved.

Responsible wagering

The app is for adults of legal wagering age and only where wagering is lawful. Set a limit before the first race and never chase a loss. For confidential help resources, visit the National Council on Problem Gambling.
