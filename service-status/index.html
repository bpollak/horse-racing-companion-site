When the app shows racing, and when it does not.

The app distinguishes scheduled seasonal gaps from data delays. Its in-app freshness label is the authority for the current card.

Current seasonal schedule

The Del Mar summer meet ended September 7, 2026.

The next scheduled Del Mar meet, the Bing Crosby Season, runs Friday, November 6 through Sunday, November 29, 2026. Between meets, live cards are intentionally unavailable while completed race days and methodology remain available. See the final tracked summer results.

Del Mar races only on its published dates, typically Thursday or Friday through Sunday plus some holidays, and is dark the rest of the week. Entries may appear before live odds. Live picks normally begin about two hours before the day’s first race; the exact expected time appears in the app.

Summer-pass access continues in November

Purchased the Summer 2026 pass? The November 6–29 Bing Crosby meet is included at no additional charge. Use Restore Purchases in the app's Settings if your pass does not appear. New meet passes are not for sale yet.

After Bing Crosby, the next regular live meet at Del Mar is summer 2027. Exact dates have not yet appeared on the official calendar.

What the labels mean

  • No racing today: no Del Mar card is scheduled for the current day.
  • Entries are coming soon: a race day is scheduled, but the public card is not yet available.
  • Live Picks Open Later: the card is available and live pick updates have not started yet.
  • Live Updates Delayed: expected fresh information is temporarily unavailable; outdated picks are hidden and complimentary access is protected.
  • Results Final: official results have been received for every race on the card.

Available every day

Open Results in the app to explore completed race days, saved picks, official finishes, and recorded misses. This path does not use a complimentary racing day.

Report a problem

If the in-app state does not match the published race schedule, pull down to refresh and then email feedback@horseracingcompanion.com with the app version, race date, and a screenshot. Replies typically arrive within two business days.
