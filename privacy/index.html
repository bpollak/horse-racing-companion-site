Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026. This policy explains the limited information Horse Racing Companion uses to provide race-day analysis and app access.

SummaryInformation collectedHow it is usedService providersRetentionYour choicesChildrenContact

Privacy at a glance

No account, advertising profile, or precise location is required.

Horse Racing Companion does not sell personal information, use third-party advertising trackers, or ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location inside the app.

Information we collect

Random installation identifier

The app creates a random identifier for that installation. It is used to remember complimentary racing days and season-pass access. It is not your Apple ID, advertising identifier, device serial number, name, or email address.

Access and product interactions

We record which racing dates an installation intentionally unlocks, the remaining complimentary-day count, entitlement status, and basic request timestamps needed to operate and protect the service.

Purchase information

If season-pass purchases become available, Apple processes the payment. Our service receives signed transaction information needed to verify and restore access, such as product, transaction, purchase, and expiration details. We do not receive or store your payment-card number.

Technical information

Like most internet services, our hosting provider may process IP address, request time, response status, and similar security or diagnostic information when the app contacts the service. We use this information to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app—not to build an advertising profile.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. Do not send payment credentials, government identification, or wagering-account details.

Race information

The race cards, odds, scratches, changes, recommendations, and results stored by the service concern public racing events and are not information about an app user.

How we use information

  • Provide current cards, recommendations, results, and access status.
  • Count complimentary racing days and restore valid purchases.
  • Protect the service from abuse, diagnose failures, and maintain reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and accessibility requests.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and App Store obligations when applicable.

We do not use app information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Service providers and disclosures

We disclose limited information only as needed to operate the app:

  • Apple: distributes the app and processes App Store purchases under Apple’s own privacy terms.
  • Railway: hosts the application service and database and may process technical request information on our behalf.
  • Professional or legal recipients: only when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the service, or complete a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and security

Access and transaction records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide complimentary-day and purchase restoration, prevent fraud, maintain the historical integrity of access records, and satisfy legal or accounting obligations. Support email is retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain an appropriate support record.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted network connections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy choices

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with an installation by emailing brettcpollak@gmail.com. Because the app does not use a login, we may ask for the installation identifier shown in the app so we can locate the correct record.

Deleting an installation record may reset complimentary-day status and may prevent the service from restoring access unless a valid App Store transaction can be verified again. Some transaction or security records may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention.

California residents may also ask what personal information we hold, request correction or deletion, and confirm that we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

Children

The app is intended for adults of legal wagering age and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

International use

The service is operated from the United States. If you use it elsewhere, information may be processed in the United States, subject to applicable law. The app is only intended for use where its informational features and any related wagering activity are lawful.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be communicated in the app or on this site when appropriate.
