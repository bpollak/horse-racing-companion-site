APP REVIEW INFORMATION

A complete path on live and dark days.

This page documents the functional boundaries and reviewer path for Horse Racing Companion. No sign-in is required.

Test stepsSeasonal behaviorPurchase flowWagering boundaryContact

Functional test steps

  1. Open Races to view the current day or the next scheduled race day.
  2. Open Results. The newest completed race day is selected automatically.
  3. Tap a completed race to compare the saved pre-race pick with the official finish, including recorded misses.
  4. Open How It Works for the methodology and limitations.
  5. Open Settings, then Season Pass, to load the submitted product, view Apple’s localized price, purchase, or restore. Support, privacy choices, and age suitability are also available from Settings.

Archived results and methodology remain available without using a complimentary racing day or making a purchase.

Expected seasonal behavior

Horse Racing Companion covers Del Mar racing. On a dark day, the Races screen intentionally shows the next scheduled race day rather than fabricated live content. During a gap between meets, use Results to inspect completed race days. Current operational context is posted on the service-status page.

Complimentary days and season pass

The first three intentionally unlocked racing days are complimentary. Afterward, open Settings and choose Season Pass to load the submitted non-renewing pass with Apple’s localized price and exact access end date. Restore Purchases is on that Season Pass screen and checks the same Apple Account. The purchase unlocks app picks; it is not a wagering deposit, balance, credit, or payment method for a wager.

Wagering boundary

The app never accepts, executes, routes, or transmits a wager.

The submitted app labels each pick by type (including Win, Place, and Show where applicable), shows pick confidence, and may display an estimated total return for the track’s minimum stake using current odds. The estimate is informational and is not a guaranteed payout. There is no adjustable stake control, teller wording, betting-window instruction, wagering account, deposit flow, wallet, virtual currency, betting-operator connection, or settlement of a user wagering account. Horse Racing Companion does not know whether a user placed a wager.

The app is rated 18+ because it concerns adult wagering subject matter. It is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Del Mar Thoroughbred Club.
